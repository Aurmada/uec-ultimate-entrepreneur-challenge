UEC 3D phone test

The game as it ships in this version, with the two heaviest fighters, Cathie and Carl, fighting on the garage stage, timed in twelve setups that each switch one thing off: the resolution, the stage light, the stage's pictures, the hit effects, and 3D against 2D. It finds what a phone spends its frame on. An older or mid-range phone tells the most.

  1. Open this page in the phone's own browser (Chrome on Android, Safari on an iPhone), with nothing else running, the battery above 30% and Low Power Mode or battery saver off.
  2. Tap Run the test, keep the screen on and leave the phone for about a minute and a half.
  3. Tap Copy results and send them on: paste them into a message or an email. Chrome on Android names the phone's model in the results.
