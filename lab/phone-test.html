UEC 3D phone test

The game as it ships in this version, with the two heaviest fighters, Cathie and Carl, fighting on the garage stage with every effect on. The 3D fighters should cost no more than about 2 ms a frame on a mid-range phone. An older or mid-range phone tells the most.

  1. Open this page in the phone's own browser (Chrome on Android, Safari on an iPhone), with nothing else running and the battery above 30%.
  2. Tap Run the test, keep the screen on and leave the phone for about 45 seconds.
  3. Tap Copy results and send them on: paste them into a message or an email. Chrome on Android names the phone's model in the results.
