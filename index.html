HOW TO PLAY

🎯 THE GOAL

Win 2 of 3 rounds. Empty your rival's health bar or have more health when the 60-second bell rings. Landing and taking hits charges your energy meter — spend it on a ⚖️ Cease & Desist (25), your ⚡ Special (50) or go full 🦄 UNICORN MODE (100) — or skip the meter entirely and 💸 Acqui-Hire their energy for yourself. And race your rival to the 💼 mystery briefcases that fall mid-round — every one hides a secret power.

⌨️ CONTROLS
On phones & tablets, on-screen pads appear automatically.
🔗 COMBOS — SUPER SIMPLE

Land a hit, then press any other attack. That's the whole system — no order to memorise, no wrong answer.

🖐/ 👊/ 👟/ 🚀×5 → ⚡

Any mix, up to 5 hits, then finish. Mash freely.

  • 🔀 Any order works. 👊👊👟, 🖐👟👊, 👟🚀👊 — all legal. There is no wrong sequence.
  • 🥊 Mashing is fine — the game waits for your hit to land before firing the next move, so you can't out-run it.
  • 🚀 Launch is your anti-air and your juggle starter: it pops them into the air, so hit them again on the way down.
  • 🏁 Finish big: cap the string with ⚡ Special, ⚖️ C&D, 💸 Acqui-Hire or full 🦄 Unicorn.
  • 💥 Later hits scale down, so a finisher beats a 6th jab.
  • 🌬️ Miss the first hit? No chain — a swing that hits nothing leaves you open, so land the opener.
📄 DEFENSE & METER

📄 NDA (hold its own button — / or Q, never the crouch key) cuts damage to 15% chip, and chip can never finish you. ▼ Crouch is separate: you duck under high attacks and can still attack from down there. You can't paper over a Hostile Takeover — jump away instead. 💨 Dash (free, short cooldown) closes gaps or escapes corners and cancels straight into attacks. PARRY: tap block at the very last instant to turn an attack away — the attacker staggers and you pocket energy. Grabs beat parries. Energy: +12 when you land a hit, +8 when you take one — spend it 💣 25 · ⚡ 50 · 🦄 100. Unicorn Mode lasts 6 seconds: +35% damage, +25% speed, no chip taken. A 🛡 Legal Shield briefcase absorbs one full hit — even a Takeover. A 📦 ring on the floor under you means Jeff Bozo has shipped you a parcel: it lands a second and a half later and jumping won't dodge it, so step off the mark or block.

⚡ SPECIAL ABILITIES
🔴 PLAY WITH FRIENDS

Live match: Challenges → Create Live Room → send the link. You'll be pinged the second your rival joins, you each lock in (the host picks the arena), and the two of you fight as yourselves in real time — rematches fire when both players accept. Async call-out: share your challenge link any time and friends battle your fighter's AI ghost whenever they open it. Every share button on the results screen doubles as a call-out — brag responsibly.

⚖️ EVERYONE FIGHTS AS THEMSELVES

You fight as your own founder by default, and you pick your rival. You can also play as any roster fighter from the select screen, and then you get that fighter's style and moves. Every player's own founder has identical stats: 1.00 speed, 1.00 power, 100 HP. Your base character, hair, headwear, eyewear, facial hair, outfit, colours and photo are pure cosmetics: they change how you look, never how hard you hit.

Stats also never grow with rank. A DECACORN and a first-day GARAGE DREAMER bring exactly the same tools. Climbing the ladder buys you reputation, not power. Only the roster fighters have varied speed/power/HP, and that's what makes each of them feel different, whether you fight them or play as them.

🏆 RANKED POINTS — TWO LADDERS

Local (vs AI): win = 20 × difficulty (Rookie ×1 · Contender ×1.5 · Champion ×2.5 · call-outs ×2) + 5 per KO round + a streak bonus capped at 15. A loss still earns +3 for showing up — very lean-startup of you. These points live on your device.

Global (🔴 LIVE only): a real win over a real human is a flat +40, +5 per KO round, + the same streak bonus; the loser gets +3. Beating the AI never moves the global board, and points only count once both players report the same result — so the ladder can't be faked.
